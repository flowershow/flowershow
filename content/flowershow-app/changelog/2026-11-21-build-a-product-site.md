Launching something? A new guide, [[build-a-product-site|Build a product site from one folder]], takes you from an empty folder to a site with a landing page, a pricing page, docs, a blog and a changelog in about 15 minutes. You design the landing and pricing pages in HTML with `layout: plain`; the docs, blog and changelog folders work with no setup. flowershow.app is built the same way.

Three use-case pages now show the range of what you can build:

- [Websites](/uses/websites): from one Markdown file to a whole site in five steps, Markdown and HTML side by side, and every website feature in one list, with Premium ones marked.
- [Product sites](/uses/product-sites): how a landing page, pricing, docs, blog and changelog fit in one folder.
- [Changelogs](/uses/changelogs): turn a `CHANGELOG.md` or a `changelog` folder into a timeline page.

**Fixes**

- Full-width hero images (`imagelayout: full`) now get a dark overlay with white text by default, so the title is readable on dark photos. Set `overlay: light` for a pale wash with dark text, or `overlay: none` to keep the old look. See [[hero-sections|Hero sections]].
