Anything you publish can be read by anyone with the link. So when your AI assistant publishes with the [Flowershow skill](/docs/agents/skills), it now tells you which files are going out, to which site, and anything it will remove. This applies to a first publish, an update, an anonymous site and the Claude/ChatGPT connector.

There's no extra confirmation step when nothing needs one. Your assistant stops and asks first only when:

- **a file looks private**, such as drafts, personal notes, or data with names or emails;
- **the publish would delete files** from your site;
- **the files aren't what you asked for**, such as extra files in the folder, or a new site you didn't ask to publish.

Update the skill to get this: re-run the install command on the [Skills](/docs/agents/skills#installation) page.
