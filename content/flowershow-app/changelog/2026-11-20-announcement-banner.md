title: Add an announcement banner to your site
date: 2026-11-20
description: Show a message in a bar above the navbar, to announce a launch or a notice. Set it in the dashboard or config.json.
showToc: false
---

You can now put an announcement bar at the top of every page of your site. Set it under **Settings → Appearance** in the dashboard, or with `banner` in `config.json`. The text can use **bold**, _italic_ and links, and visitors can close the bar unless you turn that off. If you change the text, it shows again for everyone who had closed it.

See [[banner|Announcement banner]] for the details.
