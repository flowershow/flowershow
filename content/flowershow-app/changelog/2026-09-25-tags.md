Flowershow now supports **Obsidian-style tags**. Tag any page and we'll build browsable tag pages for your whole site — no configuration needed.

**Two ways to tag.** Declare tags in frontmatter, inline in the body, or both — they're combined into a single tag set for the page:

```yaml
---
tags: [book, book/fiction]
---
```

```md
Reading notes on my favourite #book and #book/fiction titles.
```

**Tag pages.** Visit `/tags` for an index of every tag on your site with page counts, and `/tags/{tag}` for a listing of every page carrying a tag. Tags are hierarchical, so `book` also surfaces `book/fiction`, and matching is case-insensitive.

**Rendered pills.** Inline `#tags` render as clickable pills, and frontmatter tags appear as a row of pills at the top of the page — each linking to its tag page.

**Now in Bases.** `file.tags` and `file.hasTag()` in [Obsidian Bases](/docs/reference/obsidian-bases) now include inline `#tags` from the body, not just frontmatter tags.

Learn more in the [Tags reference](/docs/reference/tags).
