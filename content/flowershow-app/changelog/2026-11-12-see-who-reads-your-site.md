You published something. Is anyone reading it? Now you can see at a glance, without setting up Google Analytics or adding a script.

- **A number on every site card.** Your dashboard shows "412 people in the last 30 days" under each site. Click it to see more.
- **An Analytics tab for each site.** People and views over 7 or 30 days, a daily chart, your top pages by title, and the sites your readers came from.
- **Nothing to set up.** It's on for every site, on every plan, from today. Counting started on 12 November, so there's no earlier history.
- **No cookies on your readers.** Visits are counted without cookies, and numbers update every 10 minutes.

The screenshot above uses sample numbers.

**Good to know**

- The numbers are estimates. People who use ad blockers aren't counted, and your own visits are included.
- Raw `.html` pages and password-protected sites aren't counted yet.
- If you already use Google Analytics or Umami, it keeps working alongside.
