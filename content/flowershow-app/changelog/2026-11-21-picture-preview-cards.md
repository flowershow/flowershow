When someone shares a link to your site in Slack, X, LinkedIn, WhatsApp or iMessage, the preview now looks more like your page.

- **Pages with an `image`** show it on the left of the card, with the title on the right. This works on every plan; before, free sites never showed page pictures.
- **Pages without one** use your site's `image`, so they still get a picture card with their own title. Before, premium sites showed that one image, without the title, on every page.
- **Pages with no image at all** get a centred title card with your site name and logo. It stays readable when WhatsApp and iMessage crop previews to a square.
- **A section label** above the title, taken from the page's folder ("Blog", "Docs"), makes previews from different parts of a site easy to tell apart.

If you made your own 1200×630 share image, it's still used as it is. See [[seo-social-metadata|SEO and social media metadata]] for how each page's preview is chosen.
