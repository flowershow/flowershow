Flowershow publishes more than Markdown: HTML pages go out as-is with their CSS, JavaScript and data, next to your Markdown pages, Obsidian Canvas diagrams and images, all from one folder. The new reference page lists which files get your site's theme, which are served as-is, the URL each one gets (`.html` URLs keep their extension) and the limits on a publish. See [Supported file types](/docs/reference/supported-file-types).

We also corrected the [Obsidian Canvas](/docs/reference/obsidian-canvas) docs: a standalone canvas page is at the file's path without the `.canvas` extension.
