Anything you publish can be read by anyone with the link. So when your AI assistant publishes with the [Flowershow skill](/docs/agents/skills), it now shows you first exactly what will go out, and waits for you to say yes. This applies to a first publish, an update to an existing site, an anonymous site and the Claude/ChatGPT connector.

- **Every file, listed:** you see the full list and the site it goes to, not just the page you were working on.
- **Removals spelled out:** an update also deletes from the site any files you've deleted locally, and your assistant tells you so.
- **Private-looking files flagged:** drafts, personal notes, and data with names or emails get pointed out, so you can leave them out.

Update the skill to get this: re-run the install command on the [Skills](/docs/agents/skills#installation) page.
