- **Publish with AI:** a new page on how to go from your AI session (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor and more) to a live website, from one page to a whole site. See [Publish with AI](/publish-with-ai).
- **Updated Flowershow skill:** your agent now installs and logs in to `fl` for you, publishes HTML pages as-is alongside Markdown, checks for site-name clashes before a first publish, and can convert `.docx`, `.pptx`, `.pdf` and similar files to Markdown before publishing. Update it with `npx skills add flowershow/skills --global`. See [Skills](/docs/agents/skills).
