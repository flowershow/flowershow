Every Flowershow site now has built-in analytics, on for every plan with nothing to set up. Open your site in the dashboard and choose the **Analytics** tab.

- See **People** and **Views** over 7 or 30 days.
- See your **top pages** and where readers **came from**.
- Get a people count on each site card in the dashboard.
- Numbers update every 10 minutes and are counted without cookies.

Your own visits are included, and the numbers are estimates that run low when readers use ad blockers. Raw `.html` pages and password-protected sites are not counted, and counting starts at launch, so there is no history. Google Analytics or Umami you set up yourself keep working. See [[analytics|Analytics]].
