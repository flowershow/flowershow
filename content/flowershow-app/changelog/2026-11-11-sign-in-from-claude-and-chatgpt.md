The Flowershow connector can now sign in to your account. Add `https://flowershow.app/api/mcp` as a connector in Claude or ChatGPT. You can publish straight away without an account; when your AI needs your account, sign in and choose **Allow**. From then on your AI publishes to your account, and the connector is no longer a beta. See [Publish from Claude and ChatGPT](/docs/agents/mcp).

- **Your sites, in any conversation:** ask for "my team site" in a new chat and your AI finds it, checks which pages it has and updates it. New sites go straight into your account, with no claim link and no 7-day expiry.
- **Edit without losing anything:** before changing a page, your AI reads its current text, so the rest of the page and the site stay as they are.
- **Sites shared with you:** if you're an [editor](/docs/reference/collaborators) on someone's site, your AI can publish to it too.
- **Keep a site you made without an account:** if you published without signing in earlier in the conversation, ask your AI to keep the site and it moves into your account.
- **You stay in control:** see the apps you've connected under **Connected apps** on the [Access tokens](https://cloud.flowershow.app/tokens) page, and revoke any of them there.

Connectors already added with `https://flowershow.app/api/mcp` or `https://flowershow.app/api/mcp/account` keep working.
