Flowershow is designed to work well with AI agents and coding assistants. Ask your AI to publish, and it turns your Markdown, HTML and converted documents into a website, then configures and styles the site for you.

## Get started

- **Claude or ChatGPT app:** add the connector URL `https://flowershow.app/api/mcp`, then ask it to publish. No account needed to try it. See [Publish from Claude and ChatGPT](/docs/agents/mcp).
- **Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and other agents with skills:** ask your agent: "Install the Flowershow skill from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/flowershow/skills/main/SKILL.md". It publishes with the [`fl` CLI](/docs/reference/cli). See [Supported agents](/docs/agents/supported-agents) for per-agent steps.

Without an account, your site gets a live link that lasts 7 days, plus a claim link to keep it.
