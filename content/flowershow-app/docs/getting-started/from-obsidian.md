---
title: Getting Started — From Obsidian
description: Publish directly from your Obsidian vault — no GitHub account needed.
---

1. Sign up at [cloud.flowershow.app](https://cloud.flowershow.app/) and click **New Site**. Pick a name, e.g. `my-notes`.
2. In Obsidian, open **Settings > Community Plugins**, search "Flowershow", install and enable it
3. Go to [cloud.flowershow.app/tokens](https://cloud.flowershow.app/tokens) and create a Personal Access Token
4. Open the **Flowershow plugin settings**, paste your token, and set **Site Name** to your site's name exactly as it appears in the dashboard (e.g. `my-notes`, not its URL)
5. Click the Flowershow icon in the sidebar, select your notes, and publish

Your site is live at `my-notes-<username>.flowershow.me`. The dashboard shows the exact address.
