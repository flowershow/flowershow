Flowershow has a sister site: **[The Way of Markdown](https://wayofmarkdown.com)**. It's been live since July and it's about time we said so here.

The premise is simple. Nobody really cares about markdown; it's like caring about ASCII. What you care about is having a website, a knowledge base, your team's docs, a catalog of your books, and owning them rather than renting them from a platform. Markdown plus its tool ecosystem gets you there from plain files. That's the way of markdown.

What's there:

- **[Markdown basics](https://wayofmarkdown.com/basics)**: learn the syntax in ten minutes.
- **[Markdown-based everything](https://wayofmarkdown.com/markdown-based)**: guides to building websites, blogs, wikis, knowledge bases and a Notion replacement.
- **[Markdown everywhere](https://wayofmarkdown.com/everywhere)**: how markdown works (or half-works) in WhatsApp, Slack, Google Docs, Notion, ChatGPT and more.
- **[The Markdown Database Pattern](https://wayofmarkdown.com/markdown-database)**: why your notes folder is already a database.
- **[The Markdown Manifesto](https://wayofmarkdown.com/manifesto)**.

Flowershow appears there as *one* way to publish, not the headline. The site is about the ecosystem, and it's honest about the limits.

It's also a Flowershow site itself: a public repo of markdown files, [on GitHub](https://github.com/flowershow/wayofmarkdown). Have a look and tell us what's missing.
