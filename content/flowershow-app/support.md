## Email

Write to **[support@flowershow.app](mailto:support@flowershow.app)** for help with your account, billing, a site that won't publish, or the Flowershow connector in Claude, ChatGPT or another AI tool. Include your site URL and what you tried.

## Community

- **Discord:** ask questions and share what you've built at [discord.gg/JChzM5VdFn](https://discord.gg/JChzM5VdFn).
- **Bugs and feature requests:** open an issue at [github.com/flowershow/flowershow/issues](https://github.com/flowershow/flowershow/issues).
