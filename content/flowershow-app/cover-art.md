title: About our cover art
description: Every post on the Flowershow blog wears a Japanese bird-and-flower woodblock print from the public domain.
---

Every post on the [Flowershow blog](/blog) wears a Japanese bird-and-flower print, mostly from Katsushika Hokusai's *Large Flowers* series and Utagawa Hiroshige's *kachō-e* (bird-and-flower pictures), made in the 1820s to 1850s. Flowers on show felt right for a tool that puts your notes on show.

All the prints are in the public domain. Most come from the [Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access](https://www.metmuseum.org/about-the-met/policies-and-documents/open-access) collection, with others from the Rijksmuseum and Wikimedia Commons. We cropped them to fit the cover format.
