## Why we'll be around

**We've built tools that outlived their first chapter.** Rufus started Open Knowledge and CKAN in the 2000s. CKAN still runs government data portals around the world. We're building Flowershow the same way: open source, plain files and a long view.

**We publish with it every day.** This page, [Life Itself](https://lifeitself.org), our notes and our reports all run on Flowershow. We're our own most demanding users.

**Your site is your folder.** Plain Markdown and HTML, in Git if you like. The site is a view of your files, which is the whole idea.

## What indie gets you

- **A free plan that stays free.** It's how most sites start, and we mean to keep it that way.
- **Features from real use.** We build what we and you need to publish. Lately: [see who reads your site](/changelog/2026-11-12-see-who-reads-your-site), [invite editors](/changelog/2026-11-09-invite-editors) and [sign in from Claude and ChatGPT](/changelog/2026-11-11-sign-in-from-claude-and-chatgpt).
- **The people who build it.** Write to us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/JChzM5VdFn) and it's usually one of us who answers.
